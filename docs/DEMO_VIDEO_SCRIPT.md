# STRATA demo script — 2 minutes 50 seconds

Prepare: open the live site and verify rankings load. Do not show private file
paths, tokens or CSV contents. Use an authorized small six-table pack within
hosted limits only if available. Show processing/results only from an actual
completed run; otherwise demonstrate validation and explain the remaining steps.

## 0:00–0:20 — The question
**Screen:** Landing page and STRATA title.

“A season average tells us what happened. But who performs when the match becomes
difficult? STRATA explores cricket performance under pressure, with evidence you
can inspect and uncertainty you can see.”

## 0:20–0:45 — Explore
**Screen:** Scroll through the landing narrative, then select Rankings.

“This interface connects to a Python analytics service. Approved rankings come
from a reviewed snapshot. The Live Analysis Console supports separate temporary
analyses of uploaded data. The visual story introduces the project; the ranking
tables show the numerical evidence.”

## 0:45–1:20 — Rankings and evidence
**Screen:** Show batting rankings, select a player row and inspect its evidence;
switch to bowling. Point to score, exposures, matches and evidence label.

“Batters and bowlers are evaluated separately against historical context.
Noisy estimates are pulled toward the cohort average. Players are ordered by
evidence tier, then pressure score, with player ID resolving ties. These top tens
are not auction recommendations. Read each score alongside exposures, matches
and uncertainty. A high position does not automatically mean strong evidence.”

## 1:20–1:50 — Methodology
**Screen:** Select Methodology. Click Source & validate, Reconstruct context,
Fit historical context and Evaluate & rank. Toggle batting and bowling.

“Six linked tables establish match context and player identity. Pressure uses
the state before each delivery, with historical thresholds fitted before the
evaluation period. Batting rewards scoring above expectation; bowling rewards
conceding less than expected. Match resampling estimates uncertainty. Here judges
can inspect the formulas, evidence requirements and limitations.”

## 1:50–2:30 — Live Console
**Screen:** Click Open Live Console. Show the six upload slots and validation
feedback. If a valid authorized small pack is available, upload all six files,
use the enabled analysis start control and show genuine stage updates. Show
results only if the job completes; otherwise keep the validation view visible.

“The Console checks schemas and relationships before analysis. Incomplete packs
cannot produce rankings. Progress comes from the backend. Completed jobs provide
batting and bowling results with player, match and delivery evidence. Uploaded
results stay separate from approved production rankings. This is temporary
analysis, not permanent storage.”

## 2:30–2:50 — Honest limits and close
**Screen:** Show the limit/session notice, then return to ranking evidence.

“Sessions last fifteen minutes. Full-pack processing on Render Free is not
certified. Auction valuations and Match Lab remain unavailable. STRATA makes
pressure performance inspectable: a ranking, its evidence, and an honest account
of what that evidence can tell us.”
